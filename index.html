Recent work

Friction removed. In production.

Four examples of automation and process improvement in practice — three of them our own SaaS products, across different industries.

Aviation · SaaS

CrewSwap

Finding cover or a swap shouldn't mean scrolling through group chats. CrewSwap gives UK airline crew a simple, direct way to post and pick up shift swaps — live today with a large base of professional pilots and cabin crew.

Construction · SaaS

SiteForge

Pre-start packs, training matrices and CHAS, SMAS and Constructionline Gold renewals are usually scattered across spreadsheets and email threads. SiteForge brings that into one workflow — in use by several UK civils and construction businesses to manage pre-start processes end to end.

Aviation · AI

Ask Atlas

Too much training knowledge lives in one experienced instructor's head, and sim time gets lost re-explaining things a trainee should already know. Ask Atlas gives crew instant, cited answers from an organisation's own training material — and gives training departments visibility into where the knowledge gaps actually are. In active pilot conversations with airlines, logistics carriers and aviation consultants.
