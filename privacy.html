Why we use it, and our lawful basis

UK data protection law says we must have a lawful basis for using your personal data. We rely on the following:

  • Replying to your enquiry. We use your details to respond to you and discuss your needs. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries about our services, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract with you.
  • Working with clients. If you become a client, we use your details to deliver the work we have agreed and to manage our relationship with you. Our lawful basis is the performance of our contract with you.
  • Meeting legal obligations. We keep some records, such as invoices and accounts, because the law requires us to. Our lawful basis is compliance with a legal obligation.
  • Running and securing the website. Technical information about visits is processed to deliver the site and protect it from misuse. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in running a secure website.

We do not use your details for marketing, and we never sell your personal data.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy.
  • Rectification: ask us to correct data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your data.
  • Restriction: ask us to limit how we use your data.
  • Portability: ask for your data in a commonly used format, or for us to send it to another organisation.
  • Objection: object to us using your data where we rely on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on your consent.

Some of these rights only apply in certain circumstances. To use any of them, email hello@tredgolddigital.com. There is no charge, and we will reply within one month.
